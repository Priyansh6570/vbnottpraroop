The Concept
Veer Bharat Nyas, an अधिष् ठान of the Culture Department, Government of Madhya
Pradesh, is dedicated to research, fellowships and study of Bharat's long
tradition: its heroes, thinkers, philosophers, rishis, saints, poets, writers, artists,
scientists and entrepreneurs, from prehistory to the struggle against foreign rule.
The platform brings the Nyas's series, events, podcasts and books into one home
of its own. It is free for everyone, supported by advertising that VBN controls,
bilingual throughout, and open to verified creators and institutions. It streams
from high-quality masters on the web and on mobile, with TV apps to follow.
Each series, its own world
Every series is different material, so each is presented on its own terms. Each is
divided by its own natural unit (a महादे व, a river, a generation, a kaand), and its
page is built around that unit. Selected series open into an interactive 3D world
of their own.
An experience with depth
The interface uses depth as well as width and height: layered key art that moves
with the viewer, scenes that come forward on focus, and smooth transitions from
page to page. Browsing and playback stay light and fast, so the platform runs
smoothly on ordinary phones and connections.
What sets it apart
• VBN always in view. The Veer Bharat Nyas logo stays in the header on
every page and sits as a small mark in the corner of the video player, even in
full screen.
• Each series, its own world. Series are explored through their own parts,
and selected series through their own 3D worlds.
• A home that follows the panchang. The home screen follows the Vikram
Samvat, the tithi and the festival calendar, bringing the right series forward
on Mahashivratri, Ram Navami, Ganesh Chaturthi and other days.
• Research alongside. Every series has its own research material, presented
beside its episodes.
• Personalities. Every rishi, saint, warrior, poet, thinker and scientist has a
page with images, short videos and a brief introduction.
• Listen mode. Any episode plays as audio with the screen off.

Creators and Institutions
The platform is VBN's, and it is also open to anyone creating serious work on
Bharat's culture, history and spiritual tradition. VBN Originals always keep pride
of place, and every contribution is verified before it is published.
Who can contribute
• Creators. Individual creators, influencers, filmmakers and scholars.
• VBN fellows. Fellows of the Nyas publish their research and films in their
own spaces.
• Institutions. Universities, research bodies, museums and cultural
organisations, including their archives.
Joining
• Separate sign-up. Individual or institution, identity and organisation details,
portfolio, and agreement to the guidelines.
• Identity check. Every creator and institution is verified before their space
opens.
• Review by VBN. An internal VBN team reviews every work before it goes
live against published guidelines: accuracy, technical quality, copyright and
content standards.
A space of their own
• Channel page. Each contributor gets a page with their banner, introduction,
series and a verified mark.
• Creator studio. Upload, guided details, review status, channel settings and
viewing figures.
• Rights. Contributors keep ownership and give VBN a non-exclusive licence.
Why creators will come
• Themed open calls. Invitations tied to the calendar and to VBN's series.
• Featured placement. The best work features on the home screen and in
festival programming.
• Commissions. Outstanding creators can be commissioned for VBN projects.

Advertising
Advertising keeps the platform free for everyone. Ads can run across all series
and every available space, and VBN decides when, where and what runs.
Sources
• Private ads. Sponsorships and campaigns sold directly by VBN, to brands,
institutions and government departments. These take priority.
• Ad networks. Google Ad Manager as the central ad server, with Google and
Meta ad networks and others filling the remaining space.
Formats
• Video ads. Before an episode and at break points within it.
• Display ads. On the home screen, series pages and event pages.
• Series sponsorship. A series presented by a sponsor, with a "सू B जन्य सू "
credit.
• Festival sponsorship. Festival programmes and home screen takeovers on
festival days.
• Live ad breaks. Inserted during VBN's live events at moments the
production team chooses.
VBN in control
A VBN ad console, part of the platform's admin, gives the team full control:
• Where. Choose placements by series, episode, page or live event.
• When. Schedule campaigns by date and festival, and set exactly where the
breaks fall within each episode.
• What. Approve every private ad before it runs, and block categories of
network ads.
• Who. Target by language, region and device.
• How often. Set frequency caps so viewers don't see the same ad
repeatedly.
• Reports. Impressions and revenue by series, placement and campaign.
Personalised advertising in India requires viewer consent under the Digital
Personal Data Protection Act, so consent is built in from the start.

Events and Live
VBN's own events stream live on the platform, and only VBN's events. Every
event keeps a permanent page with its posters, photos, videos, podcasts and
recordings.
The events page
• Timeline. All events arranged in order, past and upcoming.
• Calendar. A switch to calendar view, following VBN's own calendar of
events.
• Event page. Posters, photos and videos from the event, its podcasts, and
the live stream when it is on.
How live works
• At the venue. Cameras and sound are mixed into a single programme feed
with VBN's on-screen graphics.
• To the cloud. An encoder sends the feed over a dedicated internet line, with
a backup connection, to a live streaming service.
• To every screen. The service prepares the stream in several qualities, and a
global delivery network carries it to viewers. Each viewer's app picks the best
quality their connection allows.
• Ad breaks. The production team triggers ad breaks at natural pauses in the
event.
• After the event. The recording is saved automatically and goes on the
event page.
• Wider reach. The same stream can go out to YouTube and Facebook at the
same time, pointing viewers back to the platform.

Design Principles
• Bilingual. Every page in Hindi or English, one language at a time, switched
with a toggle. No English placed beside Hindi as a translation.
• VBN always in view. The logo is visible on every page and in the player.
• Modern and professional first. Contemporary, polished and structured.
• Depth. Layered, three-dimensional motion and transitions, with 3D worlds
for selected series.
• Easy on the eye. An experience viewers do not forget.
• No em dashes.
• No emojis.
• No boxy design.
• No common AI components. No dot pills, no eyebrow text, no boxes with a
coloured left border.
